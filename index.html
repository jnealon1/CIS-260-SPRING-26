About Me

After experiencing a cyber-related scam in which individuals impersonated a well-known company and stole funds, I became deeply interested in understanding how such attacks occur and how they can be prevented. What began as a challenging experience quickly evolved into a strong motivation to learn more about cybercrime and digital security. In 2024, I returned to school to formally pursue cybersecurity. Since then, I have been dedicated to building my knowledge in areas such as network security, threat analysis, and ethical hacking. I enjoy learning Linux, Python, and cloud infrastructure while continuing to build technical skills. I am committed to applying my skills and experience to enhance digital security and support individuals and organizations in protecting against evolving cyber threats.
